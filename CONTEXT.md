# Travel Planner

An AI-driven travel itinerary planning workstation for exploring, designing, and refining multi-day road trips and vacations.

## Core Concepts

**Trip**:
A complete travel plan with a title, destination, date range or duration, containing a sequence of days and unassigned stash items.
_Avoid_: Plan, journey, tour, guide

**Day**:
A single calendar day or sequential stage within a trip, containing an ordered sequence of activity items.
_Avoid_: Stage, date, schedule-column

**Activity Item**:
A discrete event or stop in a day, categorized as attraction, transit, dining, accommodation, or note.
_Avoid_: Card, point, stop, event

**Leg**:
The transit segment connecting two consecutive activity items, carrying estimated or verified driving distance and duration.
_Avoid_: Route segment, gap, transit-line

**Stash**:
A collection of candidate places and inspirations associated with a trip that have not yet been scheduled into a specific day.
_Avoid_: Wishlist, inbox, backlog, pool

**Trip Cover**:
A curated landscape photograph and visual theme associated with a trip based on destination keywords.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, banner, wallpaper

## Planning & AI Collaboration

**Round**:
A single conversational interaction between the traveler and the AI planner, categorized into clarify, plan, patch-explain, or qa.
_Avoid_: Turn, dialogue, prompt-response

**Clarify Question**:
A single targeted question asked by the AI with clickable option chips to pin down a critical travel constraint before generating a plan.
_Avoid_: Interrogation, prompt, survey

**Geographic Reality Check**:
A model or server-side assessment verifying whether transit distances and travel times between consecutive stops remain feasible within a single day.
_Avoid_: Distance warning, schedule audit

**Proposal**:
A structured set of proposed itinerary modifications generated by the AI that requires traveler confirmation before being committed.
_Avoid_: Draft, suggestion, diff

**Patch**:
An atomic sequence of operations that creates, updates, moves, or removes days and activity items in a trip.
_Avoid_: Mutation, batch-update, delta

**Link Parsing**:
The process of extracting structured travel days and activity items from an external social media post, article, or travel guide.
_Avoid_: Scraping, import, crawler
